# Top 10 ADV OU Sample Teams for Beginners

**Source:** Official Smogon ADV OU Sample Teams Thread
**URL:** https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/adv-ou-sample-teams-and-team-dumps.3687813/

## 1. **Big 5 + Starmie (Beerlover)** - TSS Balance
**Author:** UD (2016-2018)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/f6229d2c867e21d6
**Team:** Skarmory / Blissey / Tyranitar / Swampert / Gengar / Starmie

**Why start here:** This is THE quintessential ADV team. The "Big 5" (Skarmory, Blissey, Tyranitar, Swampert, Gengar) is the foundation of ADV OU. This teaches you:
- **Spikes stacking** (Skarmory)
- **Spinblocking** (Gengar)
- **Sandstorm pressure** (Tyranitar)
- **Defensive synergy** (Swampert + Blissey)
- **Offensive Starmie** for speed control

**Key Concept:** Toxic + Sandstorm + Spikes (TSS) - wear opponents down through passive damage

---

## 2. **Forretress Gengar Dug** - TSS Balance with Trapping
**Author:** M Dragon (2015-2020)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/a04c29cf769e9a11
**Team:** Tyranitar / Forretress / Dugtrio / Gengar / Swampert / Blissey

**Why this team:** Learn **Arena Trap trapping** - the most controversial mechanic in ADV
- **Dugtrio** traps and eliminates Celebi, Jirachi, Tyranitar
- **Forretress** provides Spikes + Rapid Spin (role compression)
- **Pursuit Tyranitar** eliminates Ghosts and Rapid Spin users

**Key Concept:** Use trapping to remove specific threats that would otherwise wall your team

---

## 3. **Special Wall-less TSS (El Clásico)** - Spikes Offense
**Author:** Hclat (2023-2024)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/9283210847f806ee
**Team:** Tyranitar / Skarmory / Swampert / Gengar / Zapdos / Aerodactyl

**Why this team:** Learn aggressive Spikes offense
- **No Blissey** - relies on pressure instead of passive special defense
- **Double Rock-types** (Tyranitar + Aerodactyl) overwhelm defenses
- **Zapdos** provides speed control and special attacking

**Key Concept:** "AeroBi" style - two Rock-types with shared checks, forcing opponents into bad positions

---

## 4. **Zapdos + CuneDol** - Stall
**Author:** ADV Community (Classic)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/f7ba5702fe856292
**Team:** Zapdos / Suicune / Blissey / Skarmory / Claydol / Dugtrio

**Why this team:** Learn defensive stall
- **Rest Zapdos** + **Thunder Wave Blissey** handle special attackers
- **Calm Mind Suicune** is the win condition with Roar
- **Dugtrio** traps and eliminates wallbreakers

**Key Concept:** Stall out opponents with passive damage, then sweep with boosted Suicune

---

## 5. **Blue Offense** - Spikeless Magneton Offense
**Author:** Noitu (2015)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/63eda9d8d491d6a4
**Team:** Suicune / Snorlax / Magneton / Salamence / Metagross / Heracross

**Why this team:** Learn spikeless offense with trapping
- Iconic **blue color palette** team
- **Magneton** traps and eliminates Skarmory
- **Lead Suicune** forces favorable early game sequences
- **Sub-Salac Heracross** cleans with Swords Dance + Swarm boost

**Key Concept:** Trap Skarmory with Magneton, then overwhelm with physical attackers

---

## 6. **Medicham Cune Offense** - Mixed Offense
**Author:** Mana (2015)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/f5a4f4f0f5dc49ce
**Team:** Medicham / Salamence / Suicune / Snorlax / Magneton / Metagross

**Why this team:** Learn lead pressure and momentum
- **Medicham lead** scares Tyranitar and creates advantageous positions
- **Lum Berry Medicham** beats Gengar
- **Calm Suicune (ManaCune)** beats Calm Mind Blissey
- **Agility Metagross** closes games

**Key Concept:** Generate early momentum with threatening lead, convert to late game sweep

---

## 7. **Jolteon Starmie Spikes Stack** - Offensive Spikes
**Author:** Kerts (2016-2024)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/dbf81d8ecae51c39
**Team:** Skarmory / Jolteon / Tyranitar / Gengar / Metagross / Starmie

**Why this team:** Learn fast-paced Spikes offense
- **Jolteon** generates momentum vs Zapdos
- **Offensive Starmie** provides speed control
- **Agility Metagross** is the late-game win condition
- **Thunder Wave Tyranitar** cripples Dragon Dance Salamence

**Key Concept:** Fast Electric-type pressure + Spikes chip + Agility sweep

---

## 8. **Smeargle Offense** - Hyper Offense
**Author:** ADV Community (Classic)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/460b8c99ee366f6c
**Team:** Smeargle / Tyranitar / Swampert / Metagross / Gengar / Aerodactyl

**Why this team:** Learn suicide lead hyper offense
- **Smeargle** sets Spikes and/or Spore turn 1
- **Dragon Dance Tyranitar** breaks physical walls
- **Liechi Aerodactyl** cleans at +1 Attack with priority
- **Endeavor Swampert** cripples opposing Pokemon

**Key Concept:** Explosive start with Smeargle, then overwhelm with setup sweepers

---

## 9. **CBMeta CroCune Skarm Beatdown** - Spikeless Balance
**Author:** ADV Community (Classic)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/e541f7be8713393c
**Team:** Metagross / Snorlax / Suicune / Claydol / Tyranitar / Salamence

**Why this team:** Learn spikeless balance fundamentals
- **Choice Band Metagross** is the Skarmory beater
- **Calm Mind Suicune** is the win condition
- **Claydol** spins away Spikes
- Multiple Pokemon bait and chip Skarmory throughout the game

**Key Concept:** Beat Skarmory through repeated pressure, not just trapping

---

## 10. **Raikou Celebi Slop** - Modern Hyper Offense
**Author:** Shitrock enjoyer / Fruhdazi (Recent)
**Pokepaste:** https://pokepast.es/8e768980fc8f3b5f
**Team:** Tyranitar / Registeel / Swampert / Celebi / Raikou / Salamence

**Why this team:** Learn modern setup-sweeper offense
- **Dragon Dance Tyranitar** should Dragon Dance turn 1 vs everything except Metagross
- **Endeavor Swampert** weakens checks and counters
- **Registeel** is the ultimate sponge with Explosion
- **Three win conditions:** Calm Mind Celebi, Calm Mind Raikou, Dragon Dance Salamence

**Key Concept:** Force trades aggressively, then sweep with whichever win condition survived
